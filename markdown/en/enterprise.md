# Corporate Sponsors and Ownership {#sponsors}

[F5, Inc.](https://www.f5.com/) is the primary maintainer, sponsor, and steward of NGINX since the [acquisition of NGINX, Inc. in 2019](https://www.f5.com/company/news/press-releases/f5-acquires-nginx-to-bridge-netops-devops) . Learn more about F5's [stewardship and governance](docs/governance.xml) of NGINX.

NGINX® is a registered trademark of F5, Inc.

# Enterprise Distributions {#distribitions}

[F5](https://www.f5.com/products#f5-nginx) provides enterprise distributions of NGINX, including [NGINX Plus](https://www.f5.com/products/nginx/nginx-plus) .

# Commercial Support {#support}

Commercial support for NGINX is available with the [Open Source Subscription Bundles](https://www.f5.com/products/nginx/open-source-subscription-bundles) from F5.

# Training and Certification {#training_certification}

NGINX training is available from [F5](https://www.f5.com/learn/training#f:@f5_primary_product=[NGINX]) .
